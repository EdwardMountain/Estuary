Estuary V1.15

by DoDo7. Copyright (c) 2026 Edoardo Montagnoli. All rights reserved.

Load the transfer function of each system as measured in Smaart 9 (.trf, or ASCII export). Estuary shows both phases before and after correction, and suggests polarity, delay and all-pass settings for the DirectOut Prodigy.MP so the two phases meet inside the chosen band.

Reading the result

  • The status box shows the largest phase offset inside the band. Up to 60° is marked Good (green); 61–90° Almost Good (orange); above 90° is red. The goal is 0°.
  • All-pass filters are placed only inside the match range. Estuary also counts the phase rotation that filters and delay add in the octave just outside the range, so it avoids needless phase turns there.
  • Phase smoothing can be changed directly on the Phase graph or in Settings.
  • Focus (on the Phase offset graph): gives strong priority to 0° offset at the chosen frequency, over a width set by Q (like a parametric EQ). The rest of the range may get worse. With Focus on, drag the pink area to move it and drag its edges, or scroll over it, to change Q. Focus is off every time Estuary opens.
  • With coherence weighting above 0%, points where the coherence of either system is below 50% are left out of the offset check.
  • Suggestions that touch only one system are preferred. Estuary only proposes both systems when it clearly helps.

Controls

  • Strength: 100% aims for full phase matching; lower values correct only part of the offset and use fewer filters.
  • Coherence weighting: the higher it is, the less low-coherence data influences the suggestion.
  • Match range: data below the "Ignore below" line and above the "Ignore above" line is left out of the calculation. Drag the dashed lines or their labels on any graph, or type the values in Settings.
  • Delay can be shown in milliseconds, metres or feet (Settings). Distance uses the speed of sound for the air temperature and humidity you set.
  • Editing any value switches to Manual. Press Auto to calculate again.
  • Every time Estuary opens, band, knobs, smoothing, filters and slots start from their defaults. Colour mode, delay units, air temperature and humidity, and the Prodigy sample rate are remembered.
  • Knobs: drag up and down, use the mouse wheel or arrow keys. Double-click resets.
  • Phase view: drag or scroll on the degree axis, or use ▲ ▼, to move the traces up or down. Click the degree axis to reset the offset. This only changes the view, not the calculation.
  • Magnitude: click the Magnitude title to open or close the graph.

Trace folders (Chrome and Edge only)

  • In Settings, under Trace folders, add the folders where Smaart saves your traces (.trf, .txt, .csv, also in subfolders). Estuary remembers them.
  • Folders in the top bar opens the newest traces, newest first. Press 1 or 2 to load a trace into System 1 or System 2. Show all lists every trace, with a search field.
  • While Estuary is open it checks the folders every few seconds: a trace saved by Smaart appears within seconds, marked NEW, and the Folders button shows how many are new.
  • Estuary only reads these folders and never writes to them. When you reopen Estuary the browser may ask you to allow access again: press Reconnect folders.
  • This needs a browser feature that only Chrome and Edge have. In Safari and Firefox the Folders button does not appear: use Load or drag files onto the System boxes.

Demo

  • Press Demo (next to ⇄ in the top bar) to try Estuary without measurements. It loads two generated systems, Demo System 1 and Demo System 2, and calculates a suggestion straight away.
  • Press ↻ for a new demo scenario. Each scenario combines different crossover filters, a small time offset, sometimes a polarity inversion, a small reflection, noise and a realistic coherence curve.
  • Demo data is generated in your browser from loudspeaker models. It is not a measurement: never load a filter exported from a demo into a real system. While a demo is loaded the status box shows DEMO, and exported file names contain DEMO.
  • Loading a real file replaces the demo in that box. Press Demo again to remove it. Estuary always opens without a demo.

Filters

  • All-pass 2nd order (IIR_ALL_PASS): 0° at low frequencies, −180° at the set frequency, −360° above. Q sets how fast the phase turns.
  • All-pass 1st order (IIR_FIRST_ORDER_ALL_PASS): 0° at low frequencies, −90° at the set frequency, −180° above.
  • Up to 6 all-pass filters per system. Estuary models them as digital filters at the Prodigy sample rate chosen in Settings. This only matters above a few kHz.
  • Delay and polarity are not part of the EQ preset: set them by hand on the Prodigy. The export window lists them.

Files

  • .trf files hold the raw data; Estuary applies its own smoothing.
  • Smaart ASCII exports are already smoothed by Smaart, so Estuary does not smooth them again. Their coherence column is often incomplete; when it is, it is ignored.
